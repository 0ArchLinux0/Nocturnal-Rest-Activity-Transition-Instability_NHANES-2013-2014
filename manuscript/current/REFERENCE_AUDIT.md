# Psychiatry Research reference audit — KAN-425

Scope: the 21 references in `manuscript/submission/psychiatry-research/Manuscript_Psychiatry_Research_FINAL.md`. Twelve focused references were added for the editorial strengthening; all are cited in the text. The nine retained references were verified in the 2026-10-16 audit; Hori, Leaning, Zhao, and the NCHS guidelines were rechecked during this pass. New-source verification was completed on 2026-10-17 against PubMed records/abstracts, publisher metadata, and the additional primary documentation identified below. Bibliographic metadata and claim support are distinguished from full-text access; no inaccessible full text is claimed to have been reviewed.

The earlier JADR source remains frozen. This audit now describes the active Psychiatry Research package, not a change to its analysis. No reference establishes clinical validity, causality, superiority, or equivalence of the present P01 measure to another sleep measure.

## Verification decisions

- The bibliography is focused on instrument/data provenance, sleep-depression background, distinct temporal constructs, wearable/digital phenotyping, and inference. All 21 entries have an in-text use; no source was added solely to increase the count.
- PubMed supplied author lists, titles, journals, publication years, volume/issue/pages or article numbers, and DOI identifiers for the journal sources except MacKinnon–White, whose metadata and narrow supported claim were verified as described above. Sentence-case title normalization does not alter meaning.
- Full-text review is claimed only where explicitly documented. General web search returned a sign-up response and was not used as evidence. A ScienceDirect request for MacKinnon–White returned 403; the accessible publisher metadata and official HC3 documentation support only the narrower claim retained.
- Additional overlapping reviews were not retained. No reference supports removed GMM, potential-landscape, thermodynamic, diagnostic, screening, or first-ever-method narratives.
- The NCHS publication-year correction is bibliographic only. KAN-173/KAN-174 results, P01 rules, cohort rules, and analysis outputs remain unchanged.
