- The PAXMIN documentation identifies the minute file as one record per minute, defines MIMS/PAXMTSM, states that PAXQFM=0 means no QC flags and values >0 indicate an invalid minute, describes PAXPREDM, and documents the PAM protocol and partial first/last collection days.

- The SLQ_H page gives the exact SLD010H question wording (“usually get at night on weekdays or workdays”) and the SLQ060 sleep-disorder-history wording.
